NP Membership Benefits

Designed for registered non-profit organizations and community development groups.

  • Free chamber membership.
  • Access to networking opportunities.
  • Participation in community programmes.
  • Access to selected training programmes.
  • Business and organizational support.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Suitable for small businesses and new entrepreneurs looking to establish themselves.

  • All NP membership networking opportunities.
  • Business directory listing.
  • Access to selected business workshops.
  • Participation in chamber events.
  • Business development information.

Silver Membership Benefits

Designed for expanding businesses seeking greater visibility and development opportunities.

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Priority business directory listing.
  • Discounted chamber event registration.
  • Business advertising opportunities.
  • Access to advanced training programmes.
  • Business networking with established companies.

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premium membership for established businesses and organizations.

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Premium advertising opportunities.
  • Business spotlight on the chamber homepage.
  • Priority invitations to special events.
  • Exclusive business networking opportunities.
  • Leadership and business development programmes.
  • Premium promotional opportunities.
