Site Name Purpose Scenarios Colors Typography Wireframe

1. Site Name

Ginsolo Ventures

The name Ginsolo Ventures represents the business and provides a simple and professional identity for the company's online presence.

Ginsolo Ventures is a granite haulage company serving customers and construction businesses in South-East Nigeria. The name was selected because it is the established business name and can be used consistently across the company's website, branding, communication, and marketing materials.

Proposed domain: ginsoloventures.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Ginsolo Ventures website is to provide an online platform where customers, contractors, builders, developers, and other construction-related businesses can learn about the company's haulage services and request construction materials and transportation services.

The website will provide information about the company's granite haulage services, available construction materials, service locations, truck fleet, contact information, and customer support.

The site will also help potential customers understand the company's services before making an enquiry. Visitors will be able to view the types of materials transported, learn about delivery services, and contact Ginsolo Ventures for quotations and service requests.

The main materials and services presented on the website may include:

  • Granite and crushed stone haulage
  • Sharp sand transportation
  • Laterite transportation
  • Construction material delivery
  • Tipper truck haulage services
  • Bulk material transportation
  • Construction site deliveries

3. Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions that potential customers may ask when visiting the Ginsolo Ventures website. These questions will help determine the information and services that should be available on the site.

Scenario 1

Where can I order granite and have it delivered to my construction site?

Scenario 2

What types of construction materials does Ginsolo Ventures transport?

Scenario 3

How can I contact Ginsolo Ventures to request a quotation for a haulage service?

Scenario 4

Does Ginsolo Ventures provide delivery services to different locations across South-East Nigeria?

4. Color Scheme

The Ginsolo Ventures website will use a professional color scheme inspired by the construction and transportation industry. The colors will provide strong contrast and help communicate reliability, strength, and professionalism.

Deep Navy Blue

#12355B

Used for the website header, navigation, major headings, footer, and important structural elements.

Construction Orange

#F28C28

Used for buttons, calls to action, important links, accents, and visual highlights.

Light Gray

#F2F4F5

Used for page backgrounds, cards, and secondary content areas.

Charcoal

#252525

Used for body text and supporting information to maintain readability.

Color Usage

Deep navy blue will establish the main corporate identity, while construction orange will draw attention to important actions such as "Request a Quote" and "Contact Us." Light gray will provide clean backgrounds, while charcoal will be used for readable body text.

5. Typography

Two fonts will be used throughout the Ginsolo Ventures website. Limiting the site to two main fonts will provide consistency while creating a clear distinction between headings and body content.

Montserrat

Use: Website title, page headings, section headings, navigation labels, and important buttons.

GINSOLO VENTURES

Open Sans

Use: Body paragraphs, service descriptions, forms, contact information, and general website content.
